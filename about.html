About me

Rayan Akhras (He/Him/His) is a Freshman BFA Theatre and Performance Major at Emerson College. He is a multi-faceted person: An actor, director, dancer, choreographer, graphic designer and more, he enjoys dabbling in all things theatre and storytelling. His most recent roles have been Collin (Poster Boy | Original Film), Jack Kelly (Newsies Jr), Melon Rind (Decomposed Theatre: The Human Trashcan), and Nick Bottom (Something Rotten Jr) as well as writing and directing his own productions.

Rayan spent his last summer working at the National Children's Theatre in Washington D.C. as a Student Teacher, helping to teach kids more about theatre and acting. He enjoys it tremendously, and cannot wait to continue in the future.
